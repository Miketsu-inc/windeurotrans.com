This notice explains how Wind Eurotrans Kft., as data controller, processes the personal data of its contracted partners, and informs prospective partners about the same.

## Legal basis and retention

We process personal data only for a specific purpose: to carry out an order. After the order is completed, the customer's data is kept in our email and forwarding software so we can stay in touch. We also keep the data if a quote we sent was not accepted. If there is no contact or new agreement within one year, we ask our partner's consent to keep the data for one more year.

Wind Eurotrans Kft. has a legitimate interest in keeping the personal data of its partners' contact persons for staying in contact and for direct business development. Partners may object to this processing at any time, in which case we stop processing their personal data.

Every employee is responsible for making sure that unauthorised people cannot see personal data during their work, and that data is stored so that unauthorised people cannot access, read, change or destroy it.

We do not pass on stored personal data to any third person or company without the consent of the person concerned, either while carrying out an order or afterwards.

We protect the data in our systems with the highest level of technical security available to us.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to withdraw your consent and to seek legal remedy. We are ready to inform you about how we process your data at any time, and to act so you can exercise these rights.
